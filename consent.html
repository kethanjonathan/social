Informed Consent for Treatment

Welcome to CareConnect Uganda. Before we begin our journey together, it is important that you understand the nature of our social support and clinical services.

1. Nature of Services

I understand that CareConnect provides medical and psychological support, which may involve discussing difficult life experiences. These sessions are designed to facilitate healing and reintegration.

2. Confidentiality & Privacy

I understand that all information shared is strictly confidential. My records will be kept securely. Exceptions to confidentiality include situations where there is a clear danger to myself or others, or as required by Ugandan law.

3. Session Recording & Documentation

I understand that sessions may be recorded for clinical record-keeping, specialist supervision, referral verification, and service quality assurance ("Prove"). I will be notified whenever a recording is active, and these recordings are protected under the same strict confidentiality standards as my written notes.

4. Online Sessions (Telehealth)

I consent to engaging in sessions via secure video/chat. I understand that I am responsible for ensuring a private and safe environment on my end for these discussions.

4. Voluntary Participation

I understand that my participation is voluntary, and I have the right to withdraw from treatment at any time without penalty.
