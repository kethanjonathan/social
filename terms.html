Terms of Service

Clear rules for a safe, respectful, and therapeutic environment.

1. Professional Use of Platform

CareConnect Uganda is a clinical social work and counseling platform. Users agree to use this platform solely for seeking or providing professional support. Any use of the platform for dating, commercial advertising, or non-clinical socialization is strictly prohibited.

2. Account Responsibility

When you create an account, you are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. You agree to notify us immediately of any unauthorized use of your account. You must provide accurate information during registration to ensure professional clinical care.

3. Prohibited Conduct (Misuse)

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for abuse. Prohibited conduct includes but is not limited to:

  • Harassment or verbal abuse of clinical staff.
  • Recording live sessions without explicit written consent.
  • Sharing clinical specialized links with unauthorized third parties.

4. Session Rules & Attendance

To maintain clinical effectiveness, users must:

  • Attend sessions from a private, quiet location for confidentiality.
  • Provide at least 24 hours notice for cancellations.
  • Respect the boundaries and professional advice of specialists.

Last Updated: February 15, 2026. By using CareConnect, you agree to these clinical terms.

Not an Emergency Service

CareConnect does not provide emergency intervention. If you are in immediate danger, please contact local authorities or call 0800 202 791.
