Fair & Accessible Support

We offer both free and paid counseling sessions.

Free consultations are available for vulnerable clients, while paid sessions support professional care and help sustain our services.

Free support is always available for vulnerable clients.

Prices are subsidised to ensure affordability for all Ugandan citizens. Financial aid is available upon request for those in extreme hardship.

Not an Emergency Service

CareConnect does not provide emergency intervention. If you are in immediate danger, please contact local authorities or call 0800 202 791.
