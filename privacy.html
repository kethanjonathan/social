Legal & Ethical Standards

Our commitment to your privacy and the absolute confidentiality of your clinical journey.

Confidentiality Statement

At CareConnect Uganda, we adhere to the highest international and Ugandan standards of social work and counseling ethics. Confidentiality is the cornerstone of our therapeutic relationship.

  • Everything you share with your specialist—whether in video, audio, or chat—is strictly protected.
  • We do not share your clinical information with family members, employers, or government agencies without your explicit written consent.
  • Exceptions: We are legally and ethically obligated to break confidentiality only if there is a clear and immediate risk of harm to yourself or others, or in cases of child abuse as per the Ugandan Children Act.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how we handle your digital data. We are committed to protecting your personal information through advanced security measures.

1. Eligibility & Age Rule

This platform is intended for users aged 18 years and above. Users below 18 must be supported through a parent, guardian or authorised organisation.

2. Safeguarding & Client Protection

We are committed to protecting all clients from harm, abuse and exploitation. Any concerns about safety, misconduct or risk will be handled confidentially and responsibly according to our clinical safeguarding protocols.

3. Information We Collect

We collect basic account information (name, email) and clinical intake data solely to provide treatment. We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Security & Encryption

All sessions use industry-standard encryption. Your clinical records are stored in a HIPAA-compliant environment, protected by multiple layers of technical safeguards.

5. Data Retention

In accordance with the National Information Technology Authority - Uganda (NITA-U) guidelines, we only keep personal information for as long as necessary to provide counseling and support services or as required by clinical licensing regulations.

6. Data Access & Complaints

You have the right to access your records or request their deletion at any time. If you are not satisfied with a service, you may submit a confidential complaint through our Complaints Portal.
